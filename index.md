## About Me

I am an MPhil student at **Tsinghua University** (School of Vehicle and Mobility, iDlab), advised by Prof. **Shengbo Eben Li**. My research focuses on **full-stack humanoid learning**: building scalable reinforcement-learning systems for embodied agents, learning foundation behavior priors (adversarial / diffusion / generative), predictive world models, and closed-loop sim-to-real deployment on **Unitree G1**.

I am the founder of [**Renforce Dynamics**](https://github.com/Renforce-Dynamics) (20+ open-source repos, 1,100+ stars) and the gait-group lead of the THMOS humanoid soccer team.

### Research Interests

- **Foundation Behavior Models** for humanoid whole-body control: adversarial and diffusion motion priors, task-priority optimization, multimodal motion tracking.
- **Predictive World Models** for embodied agents: video-conditioned inverse dynamics, VLM-based reasoning under egocentric POMDP.
- **Self-Improving RL**: jump-start initialization, self-evolving motion priors, lifelong policy distillation.
- **Cross-Embodiment Sim-to-Real**: morphology-agnostic latent-to-latent policies, scalable training infrastructure (IsaacLab / IsaacSim / mjlab / Genesis).
